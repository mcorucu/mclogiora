# Exclusion Policy

Phase 05 defines calm free-version boundaries for content and taxonomy support.

## Included In Free Foundation

Prepared for future workflows:

- Posts.
- Pages.
- Public custom post types.
- Categories.
- Tags.
- Public custom taxonomies.

## Excluded From Free Foundation

Excluded from the free foundation:

- WooCommerce products.
- WooCommerce orders.
- WooCommerce coupons.
- WooCommerce-specific post types.
- WooCommerce product categories, tags, attributes, and other WooCommerce taxonomies.
- LMS course, lesson, quiz post types where detected.
- LMS-specific taxonomies where detected.

## User-Facing Tone

The admin UI may state:

> WooCommerce and LMS support will be handled by future premium add-ons.

This must remain informational. No purchase links, aggressive upsells, blocking nags, tracking, telemetry, or remote checks are allowed.
